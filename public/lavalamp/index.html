Lava/ Lamp.

    Edward Craven Walker invented the lava lamp in 1963. An incandescent bulb warms wax that is a touch denser than the liquid around it; warmed, it expands and rises, then cools near the top and sinks. A real one takes roughly an hour or more to get flowing; this one is sped up to about 20 seconds.
